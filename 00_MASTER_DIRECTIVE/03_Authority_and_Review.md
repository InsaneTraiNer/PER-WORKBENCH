# PER Workbench — Authority and Review

## 1. Non-Negotiable Separation of Responsibilities

The PER Workbench maintains an uncompromising distinction between intermediate AI-assisted analytical activities and authoritative engineering review. The operational tiers are defined as follows:

1. **AI-Generated Work**: Machine-generated syntheses, narrative drafts, mathematical computations, and preliminary structural proposals. All AI-generated text and data are provisional working artifacts possessing zero inherent regulatory or engineering authority.
2. **Evidence**: Primary source materials, empirical measurements, regulatory statutes, certified master plans, utility billing records, and documented site conditions cataloged with clear provenance.
3. **Analysis**: Bounded, reproducible technical calculations and qualitative comparative assessments that explicitly declare all inputs, assumptions, and uncertainties.
4. **Professional Review**: Systematic examination, verification, and critical evaluation conducted by qualified human domain experts and licensed professionals.
5. **Final Authority**: The exclusive statutory prerogative of licensed Professional Engineers (PE) and authorized municipal/regulatory officials to approve, seal, certify, and adopt engineering documents.

---

## 2. Explicit Limitation of AI Authority

**Artificial Intelligence does not independently establish final engineering judgment, professional approval, regulatory determinations, or final design decisions.**

No output from any language model, agentic script, or automated tool within the PER Workbench may be represented as a certified engineering deliverable, an official compliance document, or a completed public works plan without undergoing formal human review and written approval by a licensed professional.
