# PER Workbench — Mission

## Mission Statement
The PER Workbench provides a structured, multi-model agentic environment for synthesizing, analyzing, and authoring defensible Preliminary Engineering Reports (PERs) for civil infrastructure and water-system projects.

## V1 Objective
The specific objective for Version 1 is to instantiate a minimal, verifiable vertical slice that demonstrates end-to-end evidence ingestion, traceability, adversarial quality assurance, and preliminary report drafting for an initial infrastructure target without architectural bloat or factual fabrication.
