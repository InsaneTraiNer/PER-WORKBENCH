## 3. System Scope & Assets

- **System Type:** Large Municipal Community Groundwater System (100% Groundwater Sourced)
- **Known Infrastructure Assets:**
  - **Active Groundwater Wells:** 58 active municipal production wells in Visalia/Goshen service area (as of CY 2024 compliance inventory)
  - **Booster Pumping Stations:** 4 primary booster stations
  - **Treated Water Storage Tanks:** 4 distribution storage reservoirs
  - **Distribution Pipeline Network:** Over 607 miles of transmission and distribution piping
  - **Treatment Facilities:** Wellhead Granular Activated Carbon (GAC) treatment plants deployed for 1,2,3-Trichloropropane (1,2,3-TCP) mitigation; on-site sodium hypochlorite disinfection at active well stations

---

## 4. Core Engineering Drivers

- **Baseline Supply Issues:** Severe historical groundwater overdraft in the Kaweah Subbasin; declining regional static water table elevations; SGMA groundwater sustainability requirements mandating reduction in net groundwater extraction.
- **Water Quality Exceedances / Contaminants of Concern:**
  - *1,2,3-Trichloropropane (1,2,3-TCP):* Historical exceedances above California Primary MCL of 0.005 µg/L (5 ppt); ongoing requirement for wellhead Granular Activated Carbon (GAC) filtration.
  - *Nitrate (as N):* Concentrations historically reaching up to 7.3 ppm (mg/L), approaching the 10 ppm acute Primary Drinking Water MCL, requiring continuous monitoring and source blending.
  - *Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substances (PFAS):* PFOA detections up to 7.4 ppt and PFOS detections up to 10 ppt, exceeding the 2024 EPA National Primary Drinking Water Regulation MCL of 4.0 ppt and triggering well offline protocols.
  - *Secondary Standards:* Localized detections of Aluminum (up to 0.3 ppm vs. 0.2 ppm SMCL) and Iron (up to 300 ppb vs. 300 ppb SMCL).
- **Infrastructure Aging & Reliability:** Ongoing pipeline replacement needs across older cast-iron and asbestos-cement distribution mains; Lead and Copper Rule Revisions (LCRR) service line inventory compliance.
- **Growth & Future Capacity Needs:** Projected urban growth per City of Visalia General Plan Land Use Element; sustaining firm pumping capacity to satisfy Maximum Day Demand (MDD) of 48.7 MGD.
