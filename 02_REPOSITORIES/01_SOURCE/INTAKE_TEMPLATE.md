## 4. Core Engineering Drivers

- **Baseline Supply Issues:** [e.g., Declining static groundwater levels, regional aquifer overdraft, seasonal well capacity deficits, loss of specific capacity]
- **Water Quality Exceedances / Contaminants of Concern:** [e.g., 1,2,3-Trichloropropane (1,2,3-TCP) exceeding 0.005 µg/L MCL, Nitrate (as N) exceeding 10 mg/L MCL, Arsenic, Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substances (PFAS)]
- **Infrastructure Aging & Reliability:** [e.g., History of distribution pipe bursts, lead/galvanized service lines, unlined finished storage reservoirs, aging electrical control switchgear]
- **Growth & Future Capacity Needs:** [e.g., Projected population expansion per General Plan Housing Element, maximum day demand (MDD) vs. firm pumping capacity shortfall]

## 5. Target Public Databases to Query

Select and document status for statutory portals to be queried by discovery models:

- [ ] **CA DWR UWMP Portal:** (Query five-year Urban Water Management Plan filings for population, gross water use, baseline supply, and conservation targets)
- [ ] **SWRCB / SDWIS Drinking Water Watch:** (Query compliance monitoring records, chemical analytical contaminant exceedances, violation histories, and lead/copper rule data)
- [ ] **DWR SGMA Portal / Subbasin GSP:** (Query Groundwater Sustainability Plan annual reports, groundwater elevations, monitoring network hydrographs, and sustainable yield allocations)
- [ ] **EPA ECHO / Safe Drinking Water Information System (SDWIS):** (Query formal federal enforcement actions, health-based violations, and historical sanitary survey summaries)
- [ ] **USGS / DWR Water Data Library:** (Query state well completion reports, geological lithology logs, regional aquifer test data, and long-term monitoring well hydrographs)
